Support

Quick answers and contact.

Need help?

  • Write to dyulianoceciliosouza@gmail.com with your device model, system version and what happened.
  • In the app, Settings > About Nuvii shows the installed version.

Frequently asked questions

  • How do I sign in? With your Apple or Google account on the first screen. There is no Nuvii password to remember.
  • Where is my data? In an encrypted database on your device. If you sync, the copy is end-to-end encrypted in your iCloud, your Google Drive or Nuvii Cloud.
  • I'm changing phones. How do I move everything? Sign in with the same account on the new device and turn on sync in Settings > Sync. You can also make an encrypted backup in Settings > Backups.
  • How do I use it on my computer? Open Nuvii web and scan the QR code with the app on your phone.
  • How do I delete my account? In Settings > Sync > Nuvii account > Delete Nuvii account. The account data leaves the server right away.
  • How do I change the language? In Settings > Language and region. Nuvii is available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
  • Is AI required? No. The whole app works without AI. If you turn it on, you choose the provider and see a preview before anything is sent.
  • Reminders don't show up. Check that Nuvii notifications are allowed in the system settings and in Settings > Alerts.
