1. Summary

  • To use Nuvii you sign in with Apple or Google. This creates a Nuvii account with your name and email, so the same data follows you on your phone, tablet and Nuvii web.
  • What you write (tasks, projects, notes, team members) lives in an encrypted database on your device. When you sync, the copy is end-to-end encrypted: neither the developer nor the cloud provider can read it.
  • No ads, no tracking and no selling of data. We do not use advertising or usage analytics SDKs.
  • AI is optional, and nothing is sent without a preview and your confirmation.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Settings > Sync > Nuvii account > Delete Nuvii account).

2. Who is responsible

Nuvii is developed by Dyuliano Cecilio de Souza, the controller of the data processed by the Nuvii account. Privacy contact (including to exercise your rights): dyulianoceciliosouza@gmail.com.

4. Who we share with

We do not sell or rent data. We only use processors needed for the service:

  • Apple and Google: sign-in, notifications, the stores and, if you choose, their cloud (iCloud or Google Drive), under their policies.
  • Hosting: the Nuvii server runs on a VPS contracted by the developer (Hostinger), with encrypted backups.
  • The AI provider you choose (optional): receives only the text you approved in the preview.
  • Authorities: when required by law.

Data may be processed outside your country, with the safeguards required by applicable law.

5. Artificial intelligence

It is optional and off by default. You choose on-device AI (nothing leaves the device) or a provider of your own. Nuvii shows a preview of what will be sent, asks for consent per provider and purpose, and no suggested action takes effect without your confirmation.

7. Security

Encrypted local database (SQLCipher) with the key in the system keychain, end-to-end encrypted sync (XChaCha20-Poly1305), HTTPS connections, optional biometric lock, generic notification text and encrypted server backups. No system is infallible: keep your device updated and protected by a passcode.

8. How long we keep data

For as long as the account exists. When you delete the account in the app, the server immediately removes the account, devices, sessions, tokens, chat profile and messages, and the spaces where you were the only member; server backup copies expire within 14 days. Access records are kept for the period required by law (in Brazil, 6 months under the Marco Civil da Internet) and as long as needed for the security of the service. Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the app; the copy in your iCloud or Google Drive can be erased in Settings > Sync.

9. Your rights

Under the LGPD, the GDPR and similar laws, you may request confirmation, access, correction, portability, anonymization or deletion of your data, learn who we share it with, and withdraw consent. In the app you can already correct and export everything and delete your account. For anything else, write to the contact in section 2; we reply within 15 days. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country (in Brazil, the ANPD).

10. Children

Nuvii is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If that happens, we will delete it once we learn about it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Important changes will be announced in the app or on the store before they take effect. The current version is always on this page, in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
