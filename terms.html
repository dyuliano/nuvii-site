1. What Nuvii is

A productivity app to organize tasks, prioritizations, projects, notes and team follow-up. To use it, you sign in with Apple or Google, which creates a Nuvii account. By using the app you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy.

3. Your data and your responsibility

  • The data is yours. Content lives in an encrypted database on your device and, when you sync, travels end-to-end encrypted: we do not have the key or access to it (the exception is shared Work spaces, described in the Privacy Policy). The Nuvii account keeps only what it needs to work (name, email, devices), as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • You can delete the account at any time in Settings > Sync > Nuvii account.
  • You are responsible for keeping your device secure, making backups and keeping the backup password: without it the backup cannot be opened, and it cannot be recovered.
  • Changing or losing your device or uninstalling the app erases the data, unless you have a Nuvii backup or sync turned on.
  • You are responsible for the content you record, especially data about other people (team, feedback). Record only what is necessary and respect the law and your organization's rules. Do not store passwords or corporate credentials in notes.

4. Sync

Sync between devices and Nuvii web use your Nuvii account and keep an end-to-end encrypted copy in your iCloud, your Google Drive or Nuvii Cloud. The key stays on your devices, protected by your account; the developer and the providers cannot read the content. iCloud and Google Drive belong to Apple and Google, under their terms; we do not guarantee the availability of those services or immediate delivery between devices. If the same item is edited on two devices, Nuvii keeps the most recent version and stores the other for a while so you can recover it. Keep a Nuvii backup as well.

5. Documents imported into Notes

You can import documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, Numbers, Keynote, OpenDocument, RTF, CSV and text, including those downloaded from Google Docs, Sheets and Slides) to read in Nuvii's reading mode. The imported file is stored only on the device where you imported it: it is not sent to the Nuvii cloud, is not part of sync between devices and is not part of the Nuvii backup. Only the document's title, keywords and folder sync; notes, mind maps and other data are saved and synced normally. On another device, the document appears in the list and asks you to pick the same file to read it there. If you delete the document, uninstall the app or lose the device, the imported file is lost: keep the original elsewhere. Reading formats other than PDF relies on a simple text conversion or on the device's own viewer and may not reproduce the original layout.

6. Paid plans

The full app on your device and sync through iCloud or Google Drive are free. Nuvii Pro is an optional subscription that adds what depends on the Nuvii server: sync through Nuvii Cloud with real-time changes, the Work workspace, Nuvii web, the API, webhooks and integrations, and AI assistants through MCP.

  • Purchase and price: sold only through the App Store or Google Play, on your store account. The price is the one the store shows before you buy, in your country's currency, with any taxes the store applies.
  • Automatic renewal: the subscription is monthly and renews by itself every month, charged to your store account, until you cancel. On the App Store, renewal is charged within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Cancel: at any time in the store (iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions; Android: Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions) or with the "Manage subscription" button in the app. Pro stays active until the end of the period already paid.
  • Refunds: requested from and decided by the store, under its rules and the law of your country.
  • When Pro ends: nothing is deleted. Your data stays on your device; Pro features stop until you subscribe again.
  • The purchase is linked to your Nuvii account and can be restored on another device with "Restore purchases". Nothing is charged without your purchase in the store.
